Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Text chat with strangers, exploring countries, auto-translate and private room links are free. VIP Pro ($0.99 for 30 days) adds live video calls, the Girls/Boys gender filter, password rooms, QR codes, photos, GIFs and stickers, and the gold crown.

How does stranger chat matching work?

Stranger chat connects you instantly with real people around the world based on your chosen country filter and interests.

How do private rooms and passwords work?

Click Create Room and share the link, it is free. VIP members can also choose a password and share a QR code. The password is never put in the link: anyone who opens it has to type the password to enter.

How do video calls work?

Video calls are a VIP feature and work in private rooms and with real matched people (in private rooms & matched stranger chats). Tap Video in the chat, the other person must accept, and the call connects directly between your devices. Both people need VIP.

Can I get VIP with Space Coins?

Yes. 2300 Space Coins unlock 30 days of VIP Pro. You can top up coins with Telegram Stars from the coin wallet, then redeem VIP in the VIP window. Redeeming again while VIP is active adds another 30 days.

How does Bitcoin VIP payment work?

Pay $0.99 in BTC and enter your transaction hash in the VIP window. The app checks it on the Bitcoin blockchain and unlocks your badge.
